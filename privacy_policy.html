FlowRead — Privacy Policy

Last updated: Nov 2026

Overview

FlowRead has no accounts, no sign-up, and no login. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal detail, and we have no way to identify you.

Your files and everything you read stay on your device. They are never uploaded, never sent to us, and never sent to anyone else.

FlowRead does send a small number of anonymous, counting-only usage events, described in full below. These contain no identifier of any kind and cannot be traced back to you or to any device.

Data stored on your device

FlowRead stores your files, reading progress, settings, reading statistics, and your choice of apps for reading reminders locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is never sent to any server. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Anonymous usage statistics

To understand whether features are working — for example, how often people finish a reading reminder rather than skipping it — FlowRead may send anonymous event counts. This is the only information FlowRead ever sends about how the app is used.

Each event contains only:

  • the name of the event, from a fixed list built into the app;
  • the calendar date — never a time of day;
  • the platform (for example, Android);
  • in some cases one small non-identifying value, such as a page count, a number of minutes, or a language code.

There is no device identifier, no user identifier, no advertising ID, no IP-based profile, and nothing that links one event to another or to you. Because the date is recorded without a time, separate events cannot be grouped into a session or a history.

The events are limited to a fixed list inside the app, which cannot be extended remotely. They are: app opened, reading reminder shown, reading started from a reminder, reading completed from a reminder, reminder skipped, free book downloaded, onboarding completed, reminder permission granted, statistics card shared, and the four purchase events named under In-app purchases below.

What is never included: the names of the apps you choose for reading reminders, your file names, your file contents, anything you read, any text you look up, the books you choose, or the app you share a statistics card to.

These events are sent on a best-effort basis only. If you are offline or the request fails, it is discarded silently and nothing in the app is affected.

You can turn this off. Settings → About → "Share anonymous usage counts". With it off, nothing is sent at all. The rest of the app is unaffected — no feature is withheld and nothing is degraded for turning it off.

Network requests

FlowRead works offline. It makes network requests only in these cases:

  • Google Fonts — loaded once on first launch to display app fonts, then cached permanently on your device. No personal data is sent.
  • Free Books library — when you choose to download a book, FlowRead fetches that one file from its public source. After that the book is yours offline. No data about you is sent.
  • URL Reader — when you paste an article URL, FlowRead fetches that URL directly from your device. No data about the request is sent to us. This feature is clearly labelled as requiring internet.
  • Anonymous usage events — as described above.
  • Google Play — for purchases and for checking which features you have unlocked.

Reading itself never requires internet. Your files are opened and processed entirely on your device.

In-app purchases and subscriptions

FlowRead offers a one-time lifetime purchase and an optional monthly or annual subscription (FlowRead Plus).

All payments are processed by Google Play. We never see, receive, or store your name, billing address, card number, or any other payment detail. Google tells the app only whether you are entitled to the paid features. Google's handling of your payment information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy, not by this one.

On your device, FlowRead stores only a few yes/no flags recording which features you have unlocked, kept in your device's encrypted app storage. They contain no receipts, no transaction identifiers, and no payment data. They never leave your device.

FlowRead sends anonymous events when a purchase completes, when a subscription starts, and when a subscription ends. As with every other event, these carry no identifier and no amount — only that it happened, and on what date.

To view, change, or cancel a subscription, use Google Play → Payments and subscriptions. Cancellation is handled entirely by Google. FlowRead does not place any step, prompt, or delay in front of it.

Permissions

FlowRead requests storage access solely to let you select and read files from your device. No file content is transmitted anywhere.

If you use the camera or photo import, images are read on your device and the text is extracted on your device. Photographs and extracted text are never uploaded.

To list the apps you can choose for reading reminders, FlowRead reads only the names of apps that have a launcher icon. It does not request permission to see your full list of installed apps.

Accessibility service

FlowRead includes an optional reading-reminder feature. If you turn it on, you choose which apps you want to be reminded in — for example a social media app you want to scroll less. To notice when one of those apps opens, FlowRead uses Android's accessibility service.

The service is deliberately built with the narrowest possible access:

  • It cannot read anything on your screen. The ability to read screen content is switched off in the service's own configuration, so it is not something FlowRead could choose to do. It never sees your messages, your passwords, what you type, or what you browse.
  • It is told only about the apps you picked yourself. Android is given that list and sends FlowRead nothing about any other app.
  • All it receives is that one of your chosen apps has come to the front. That information is used immediately to decide whether to offer you a page, and is never stored, never logged, and never transmitted.

The feature is off until you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time in FlowRead's settings or in Android's accessibility settings. FlowRead does not use the accessibility service for any purpose other than this feature.

Usage access (optional)

If you grant usage access, FlowRead uses it for two things: to confirm you are still in the app before showing a reminder, so it does not interrupt you about an app you have already left, and to show you how long you have spent in that app today. This is read at the moment it is needed and immediately discarded. It is never stored or transmitted.

This permission is optional. Declining it does not disable reading reminders; it only makes them slightly less precise, and FlowRead explains the difference in settings.

Children

FlowRead does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.
