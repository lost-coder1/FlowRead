<en-US>
• "Page" mode is now the default, and it opens instantly even on very long books.
• Books now show real paragraphs instead of one block of text, with chapter titles as headings.
• The chapter index finds chapters properly now.
• Reaching the last page marks a book as read.
• The free library has grown to 150 books.
• Books from the library now share with their real cover.
• FlowRead now sends anonymous counts of which features are used — never your files, your reading or anything that identifies you. Turn it off any time in Settings → About.
