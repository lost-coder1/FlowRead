<en-US>
• Free Books downloads now show live progress (percentage or MB) so you can see something is happening.
• Sepia theme now renders Page mode on a warm paper background instead of dark — matches the other engines.
• Loading messages with long book titles no longer touch the screen edges or break at awkward places.
• Page mode loading label is clearer: "Creating Pages" instead of "Paginating".
